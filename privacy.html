Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

About this site

HOURS is a hypothetical company, and this site is a prototype made for a university class, ENTR 222. It is not a commercial product.

What we access

If you choose to connect your Google Calendar, you sign in with Google and grant read-only access to the events on your primary calendar. The page requests your events for the next 7 days. It cannot create, change or delete anything.

How your data is used

Your events are used only to show you your upcoming week and flag clashes between events on this page.

That all happens in your browser. Your events and access token are not sent to a server of ours, because we don’t have one. They aren’t saved, shared or sold, and they aren’t used for advertising or to train AI models. They are gone when you close or reload the tab.

HOURS’ use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services

Sign-in and calendar access are provided by Google. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts and its sign-in script from Google, so those companies may receive technical information such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply. This site sets no analytics of its own.

Removing access

Use the Disconnect button on the page, or remove HOURS at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the project’s GitHub page.
